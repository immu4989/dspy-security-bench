# v0.10.0 — RepeatControlTwin paired policy evidence

One favorable policy-off/policy-on comparison is not enough for a stochastic
agent. DSPy Security Bench v0.10.0 repeats the complete ControlTwin experiment,
preserves every child report, alternates condition order, and exposes
uncertainty, effect stability, mission recovery, utility, and cost as distinct
evidence.

## Highlights

- `impact control-repeat` gives every case and condition a fresh agent and
  alternates baseline-first and controlled-first trials.
- Wilson confidence intervals cover harm containment, policy-on harm-free
  outcomes, safe mission recovery, clean-utility preservation, harm
  introduction, and recovery gaps with explicit conditional denominators.
- Exact two-sided McNemar evidence summarizes prevented versus introduced
  paired functional harms without a large-sample approximation.
- Per-pair effect classes reveal stochastic instability, residual harms, clean
  regressions, and contained missions that fail to recover safely.
- Independent lower-confidence-bound and instability gates make CI conservative
  even when the observed point estimate is perfect.
- Reports preserve separated policy-off/policy-on provider usage, estimated
  cost, every nested ControlTwin report, normalized policy identity, and a
  canonical SHA-256 digest.
- `impact control-repeat-verify` recomputes every child report, schedule,
  transition, interval, exact test, aggregate, and digest offline.
- Schema-v1 JSON and SARIF distinguish residual harm, variable effects, clean
  utility regressions, and mission-recovery gaps.
- The interactive dashboard now includes a responsive repeated-evidence lab
  with an alternating schedule, interval bands, paired transitions, and honest
  inference boundaries.

## Zero-cost demonstration

```bash
pip install dspy-security-bench==0.10.0
dspy-security-bench impact control-repeat-demo --trials 5
```

The deterministic fixture prevents 25 of 25 observed baseline harms, preserves
25 of 25 baseline clean successes, and safely recovers 15 of 25 attacked
missions. The 95% Wilson lower bound for harm containment is 86.7%; the safe
mission recovery interval is 40.7%–76.6%.

The fixture is not a model result. Its repeated observations cover five frozen
synthetic procurement pairs, not unseen tasks, provider revisions, production
loss, compliance, or certification. Nominal Wilson and McNemar calculations
treat pair-trial executions as exchangeable; shared provider conditions can
violate that assumption.

Read the [RepeatControlTwin methodology](../repeat-control-twin.md) for agent
integration, CI gates, evidence verification, statistical interpretation, and
limitations.
